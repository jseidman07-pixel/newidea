# AI Internship Accelerator

## Concept

A productized service for finance and business students that helps them land internships faster using AI-assisted resume tailoring, outreach scripts, company research, application tracking, and weekly accountability.

## Why it fits

- Strong founder overlap with finance students and internship anxiety
- Zero-budget friendly because it can start as a service
- Easy campus distribution through Auburn, sorority, and finance peers

## First offer

"I will help you apply smarter to 20 finance or real-estate internships this month with custom resume bullets, networking messages, and a tracking dashboard."

## Monetization

- Done-for-you package: $99 to $299
- Group cohort: $49 to $99 per student
- Later: subscription toolkit, templates, or mini-course
