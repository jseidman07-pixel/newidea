# Real Estate Comp Pack Service

## Concept

An AI-assisted research service that produces polished property comp packs, pricing narratives, investor briefs, neighborhood snapshots, and seller-ready visuals for small agents or investors who do not have analyst support.

## Why it fits

- Strong overlap with real estate, data, research, and making things look organized
- Easier to scope than a full valuation product
- Can be sold as a premium deliverable rather than software

## First offer

"Send me the property and I’ll return a clean comp and pricing brief you can use with clients."

## Monetization

- Per report: $75 to $250
- Rush fee for same-day turnaround
- Retainers for repeat clients
