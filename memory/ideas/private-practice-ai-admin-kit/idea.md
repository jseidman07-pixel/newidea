# Private Practice AI Admin Kit

## Concept

An AI-enabled admin and content service for solo therapists, doctors, or small practices focused on non-clinical workflows like FAQs, intake reminders, website copy, newsletter drafts, and patient education content.

## Why it fits

- Access to medical and therapy contexts through family
- Real admin pain and willingness to pay
- Lower speed because trust, compliance caution, and credibility matter

## First offer

"I’ll clean up your non-clinical communication workflows and turn repeated admin questions into reusable systems."

## Monetization

- Setup fee: $300 to $1,200
- Monthly support: $200 to $500
- Could specialize in one practice type later
