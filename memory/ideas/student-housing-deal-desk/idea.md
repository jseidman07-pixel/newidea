# Student Housing Deal Desk

## Concept

An Auburn-focused housing decision service or lightweight tool that helps students and parents compare apartments, lease terms, roommate cost splits, move-in logistics, and hidden fees in one clean dashboard.

## Why it fits

- Local campus wedge
- Strong organization and research fit
- Can begin as concierge research before becoming software

## First offer

"I’ll help you compare Auburn housing options and make the cheapest clean decision without missing lease traps."

## Monetization

- One-time package: $49 to $149
- Referral or partner revenue from apartments if compliant
- Later: campus expansion and software layer
