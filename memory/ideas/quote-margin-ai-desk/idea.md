# Quote & Margin AI Desk

## Concept

A service for home-service and project-based local businesses that cleans up quoting, job-costing, and follow-up workflows. The core promise is better quote accuracy, clearer margins, and faster customer follow-up using AI plus spreadsheet/data systems.

## Why this is better

- It is tied directly to margin, not vanity metrics
- It solves a problem owners already say they have
- It is highly data-driven and operational
- It can be sold before any software exists

## First offer

"I’ll audit your estimates, show where margin is leaking, and build a cleaner quote + follow-up system."

## Monetization

- Audit: $300 to $750
- Setup project: $750 to $2,000
- Ongoing monthly dashboard/reporting: $200 to $500
