# Auburn Student Housing Intelligence Desk

## Concept

A data and research service for Auburn-area student housing operators, apartment marketers, and leasing teams. It combines competitor pricing snapshots, student renter interviews, sentiment analysis from reviews/social, and weekly “what students actually care about” briefs.

## Why this is better

- It uses your campus access as a true wedge
- It is behind-the-scenes and data-heavy, which fits you better than consumer-facing content
- It sells to businesses with budgets, not price-sensitive students
- It can start as a report service and become software later

## First offer

"I’ll deliver a monthly Auburn renter intelligence brief: competitor pricing, what students are saying, what amenities actually matter, and what messaging is likely to convert."

## Monetization

- Pilot report: $250 to $500
- Monthly brief: $500 to $1,500
- Custom research sprint: $1,000+
