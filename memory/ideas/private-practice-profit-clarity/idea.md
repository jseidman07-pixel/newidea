# Private Practice Profit Clarity

## Concept

A non-clinical analytics service for therapists or small practices that turns bookkeeping, caseload, cancellation, and payout data into monthly profitability and admin-friction insights.

## Why this is better

- Better wedge than generic admin help because it targets money clarity
- AI usage for financial forecasting is still early in this segment
- Recurring reporting could be valuable if trust is established

## First offer

"I’ll give you a monthly practice clarity report showing what’s profitable, what’s draining time, and where money is getting stuck."

## Monetization

- Initial setup/report: $250 to $500
- Monthly ongoing: $150 to $400
