# Consumer Insight Brief Studio

## Concept

A research service for small brands that converts comments, reviews, Reddit threads, TikTok conversations, and competitor content into actionable insight briefs about messaging, customer objections, and emerging content angles.

## Why this is better

- Directly matches your research strength
- Strong macro signal around the “insight gap”
- Harder to sell at first because the outcome is insight, not immediate operational ROI

## First offer

"I’ll analyze what your audience is actually saying online and turn it into a messaging and product insight brief."

## Monetization

- One-off brief: $250 to $700
- Monthly recurring insight reports: $300 to $1,000
