# Finance Student Opportunity Radar

## Concept

A curated opportunity feed for finance students featuring internships, leadership programs, scholarships, competitions, networking events, and recruiting calendars, delivered with AI summaries and action checklists.

## Why it fits

- Strong research and organization fit
- Easy content-to-product bridge
- Demand exists, but monetization is weaker unless bundled with services

## First offer

"Get a weekly shortlist of real finance opportunities with deadlines, fit notes, and what to do next."

## Monetization

- Subscription: $5 to $15 per month
- Bundle with application support or resume review
- Sponsor or affiliate revenue later
