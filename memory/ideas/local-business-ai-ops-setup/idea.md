# Local Business AI Ops Setup

## Concept

An AI setup and cleanup service for Auburn-area small businesses that want help with inbox automation, FAQ bots, content repurposing, admin systems, and workflow organization but do not want to hire an agency.

## Why it fits

- Strong macro demand signal
- Good for a founder who learns tools fast and can productize delivery
- Harder founder fit than real estate or student niches because the wedge is broader

## First offer

"I will save your business time every week by organizing one messy process with AI."

## Monetization

- Setup fee: $250 to $1,000
- Retainer: $150 to $500
- Upsells by workflow category
