# Real Estate AI Content Studio

## Concept

A done-for-you content service for real estate agents that turns listings, market updates, and neighborhood data into social posts, email newsletters, carousel content, and listing copy using AI plus human polish.

## Why it fits

- Matches real-estate interest, internship exposure, and aesthetic/organization strengths
- Strong market signal because agents are already using AI for content but still need reliable output
- Can start with one agent and expand through referrals

## First offer

"For one monthly fee, I turn your listings and local market updates into a week of polished content."

## Monetization

- Setup fee: $150 to $300
- Monthly retainer: $300 to $800
- Add-ons: listing packets, open house posts, neighborhood guides
