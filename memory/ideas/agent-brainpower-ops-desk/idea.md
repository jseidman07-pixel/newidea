# Agent Brainpower Ops Desk

## Concept

A behind-the-scenes analyst service for real estate agents that turns scattered CRM exports, listing activity, comps, seller updates, and marketing data into one clean weekly action packet.

## Why this is better

- More differentiated than “AI content”
- Solves the exact “too many tools” and “brainpower” problem agents describe
- Uses your real-estate/data fit without requiring you to be the on-camera face

## First offer

"Once a week, I turn your messy data into a clean seller report and next-action dashboard."

## Monetization

- Setup: $150 to $300
- Monthly retainer: $300 to $900
- Premium comp/seller packet add-ons
